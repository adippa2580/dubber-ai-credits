How Dubber AI Credits Work with Dubber Notes

Dubber Notes turns your calls and meetings into clear summaries, actions and insights. The AI behind it runs on Dubber AI Credits. This guide explains what you get with your plan, how AI Credits are used, and when an AI Credit Pack is worth adding.

The quick version

  • Your Dubber Notes plan includes a monthly supply of Dubber AI Credits for every user.
  • AI Credits are used as Dubber analyses your audio and writes your Notes. The more you ask a Note to include, the more AI Credits it uses.
  • AI Credit allowances and usage, including any included tokens bundled with Dubber SKUs, are pooled across your account, so lighter users balance out heavier ones.
  • The Dubber Notes Standard plan covers an indicative 7 hours of Analysed Audio per user each month.
  • If you do need more, you can top up with an AI Credit Pack at any time. AI Credits bought in a pack roll over for as long as your agreement runs.

What Dubber AI Credits are

Think of Dubber AI Credits as the fuel for Dubber's AI. Each time Dubber transcribes a call, writes a Note or picks out a key moment, it uses a small number of AI Credits. Usage is measured per hour of Analysed Audio, which is simply the audio Dubber has transcribed and analysed for you.

Each plan's AI Credits cover an indicative 7 hours of Notes per user each month.

Recording works differently on the two plans:

  • Dubber Notes Standard includes recording as part of the plan, for the same 7 hours.
  • Dubber Notes Compliance is an AI add-on and does not include recording. Recording comes from Dubber Unified Capture, which has the standard Fair Use allowance of 10 hours per user each month. The 7 hours applies to your Notes, not to your recording.

A few things to know about your included AI Credits:

  • They are pooled. Allowances, usage and any included tokens that come with Dubber SKUs are pooled at account level, so every user's AI Credits go into one pool. A team of 10 on Notes Standard has 111,000 AI Credits to share each month.
  • They are a set amount each billing period. Your plan comes with a limited allowance, and it can be used up.
  • They do not carry over. Any unused allowance expires at the end of each billing period.

The Dubber Notes Standard plan

The Dubber Notes Standard plan covers an indicative 7 hours of Analysed Audio per user each month. That includes your call recording, transcription and Notes, powered by the 11,100 AI Credits that come with each user.

You pay for your plan and nothing else, as long as your account stays within those 7 hours per user.

How far your included AI Credits go

Because allowances and usage are pooled, what matters is the average across your account, not any one person.

Average hours per user each monthDubber Notes Standard plan
Up to 7 hoursCovered by your plan
More than 7 hoursThe extra hours use additional AI Credits

Where recordings go beyond 7 hours, AI Credits are deducted at the recording rate of 1,111 per hour. Notes on those extra hours use AI Credits in the usual way, so each extra hour uses about 1,889 AI Credits on Notes, or 2,689 on Full Notes. Once your included allowance is used up, an AI Credit Pack covers the rest.

The Dubber Notes Compliance plan

Dubber Notes Compliance adds AI to your Dubber Unified Capture recording. The two parts have separate allowances:

  • Notes. The 11,700 AI Credits that come with each user cover an indicative 7 hours of Compliance Notes each month, including Actions and Complaints.
  • Recording. Dubber Unified Capture has the standard Fair Use allowance of 10 hours per user each month.

When you might need an AI Credit Pack

An AI Credit Pack gives you extra AI Credits to draw on once your included AI Credits for the month are used up. You are most likely to want one if:

  1. Your team spends a lot of time on calls. If your account averages more than 7 hours per user each month, a pack covers the difference.
  2. You move busy teams from Notes to Full Notes. Full Notes adds Actions and Complaints, which roughly doubles the AI Credits used per hour.
  3. You add Moments. Moments such as Sales Close, Sales Process and Customer Satisfaction give you deeper insight and use between 27 and 724 AI Credits per hour each. If you are on Notes, there is usually room in your plan for one or two. For Comprehensive AI, plan on a pack.
  4. You use Insight Agent or custom triggers. These use AI Credits based on how much you use them, so they vary from month to month. A pack gives you a comfortable buffer.
  5. You have busy seasons. Included AI Credits do not carry over from a quiet month to a busy one. AI Credits from a pack do, so one pack can see you through several peaks.

What makes packs flexible:

  • They roll over. Unused AI Credits from a pack roll over for as long as your agreement runs.
  • They are pooled. Pack credits sit in one shared balance for the whole business, so every user on your account can use them.
  • They work across Dubber AI. Use them for Notes, Moments, Insight Agent and any other AI or conversation intelligence feature you have chosen to switch on, until the pack is used up.
  • Bigger packs cost less per AI Credit. The Enterprise pack works out about 30 percent cheaper per AI Credit than the SMB pack.
  • Top up as you go. Buy only what you need, when you need it, and unused AI Credits stay in your shared balance for as long as your agreement runs.

Getting more from your AI Credits

You are in control of how your AI Credits are used. A few simple choices make them go further:

  • Choose who gets Notes. Create Notes for the teams and people who will use them.
  • Use Full Notes where it counts. Keep Actions and Complaints for teams that follow up on calls, such as sales and service. Notes on its own uses about half the AI Credits.
  • Use filters. Skip very short calls, personal conversations and pre-recorded messages so Notes are only written for conversations that matter.
  • Use triggers. Ask for a Note only when something important comes up, such as a pricing discussion or an unresolved complaint. Fewer Notes means fewer AI Credits, although checking for triggers uses some AI Credits itself.

Keeping track

You can see exactly where your AI Credits are going at any time:

  • Live balance. Check how many AI Credits you have left and what they have been used for.
  • Monthly statement. See your opening balance, any top-ups, usage by feature and your closing balance.
  • Email alerts. By default, you get a message when 75%, 50% and 25% of your AI Credits remain, and again when they reach 0%. You can also set alerts for a number of AI Credits used, or for a balance level you choose. Alerts can go to more than one person.
  • At zero balance. When your AI Credits run out, AI processing pauses until more credits are added.
  • Easy top-ups. Add an AI Credit Pack in a few clicks whenever you need one.

Common questions

Do my AI Credits expire?

The allowance included with your plan is for the current billing period, and any that is unused expires at the end of it. AI Credits you buy in a pack roll over for as long as your agreement runs.

Does each user have their own AI Credits?

No. All AI Credits, including the tokens bundled with Dubber SKUs, are pooled across your account, so your team shares them.

Can I change what my Notes include?

Yes. You can add or remove Actions and Complaints, change who receives Notes, and adjust filters and triggers at any time.

Which pack should I choose?

If you only go over occasionally, start with a smaller pack. If you expect to use extra AI Credits every month, a larger pack costs less per AI Credit, and unused AI Credits roll over for as long as your agreement runs.

How do I avoid running out?

Set a low balance alert. You will get an email in good time to top up.

The details

  • Indicative values. All AI Credit values quoted in this guide are indicative only. They are based on estimates at the date of publication and are subject to change.
  • Pooling. AI Credit allowances, usage and included SKU tokens are pooled at account level.
  • Fair Use Policy. This means the number of hours stated in your pricing plan. Where a plan does not state its hours, the allowance is 10 hours per user per month. Beyond that, additional charges apply or a higher plan is required.
  • Dubber Notes and Dubber Notes Compliance. Both plans include a limited AI Credit allowance, which can be used up. Unused allowances expire at the end of each billing period.
  • Recording. On Dubber Notes Standard, where recordings exceed 7 hours, AI Credits are deducted at the recording rate. On Dubber Notes Compliance, recording is provided by Dubber Unified Capture under the standard Fair Use allowance of 10 hours per user per month.
  • AI Credit Packs. Packs can be used across all the AI and conversation intelligence features you have chosen to enable, until the pack is used up. AI Credits bought in a pack sit in one shared balance for the whole business and roll over for as long as the agreement runs.

Prices are recommended retail prices in US dollars and may vary by provider and region. Plan prices are per user per month. Current as at 21 September 2026.
