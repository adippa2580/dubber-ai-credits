1Introduction

Verizon asked us for gridwall and product page content for the Dubber SKUs going onto Verizon Business Marketplace. Every tile, section and field below follows the structure of existing Marketplace partner listings, so the Marketplace team can drop the Dubber content into the same templates.

The SKU part numbers, product names, descriptions, units and token inclusions come from our Dubber SKU exhibit. Prices shown are list prices in US dollars. The product copy and usage detail come from our guide, How Dubber AI Credits Work with Dubber Notes. Anything we still need to agree, mostly Verizon's own catalog IDs and terms, is marked [TBD] and listed in section 8.

I've also included the flow that drives repeat revenue. When an account's AI Credits run low, the Dubber Notification Centre emails the people the customer has chosen and sends them to the Dubber listing on Marketplace to buy another AI Credit Pack. Once Verizon fulfils the order, the credits go back into the account's pooled balance.

A note on terms: the SKU exhibit says "tokens" and the customer-facing guide says "AI Credits". They mean the same thing. I use AI Credits in customer copy and keep tokens where the SKU definition uses it.

What's covered. The Marketplace SKUs are three plans (Dubber Notes, Dubber Unified Capture and Dubber Notes - Compliance AI) and four AI Credit Packs. The page follows the Marketplace listing format: gridwall tiles, plan selector, Overview, Benefits, Compare plans, Use Cases and Resources.

2Gridwall tiles

On the Marketplace gridwall, each partner gets one tile per product group. A tile has an optional promo banner, the logo, the group name, a one-line description of about 100 to 120 characters, a "Starting at" price line, the line "Plus Applicable Taxes and Fees", and the filter tags (Category and Solution type). The first tile is the recommended Dubber group tile. Below it are the SKU tiles in two groups: the three plans, then the four AI Credit Packs.

Overview

Dubber captures your business calls and turns them into something you can use. Dubber Notes gives every user call recording plus AI, with transcription, speakers, chapters and a clear Note you can act on, on the web or in the mobile app. For regulated teams, Dubber Unified Capture provides multi-channel, compliance-grade capture across voice and unified communications, and Dubber Notes - Compliance AI adds Notes with Actions and Complaints and access to every Dubber AI feature.

Dubber's AI runs on AI Credits. Both Notes plans include a monthly allowance for each user, covering an indicative 7 hours of Notes, and credits are pooled across your account so lighter users balance out heavier ones. If your team needs more, add an AI Credit Pack at any time. Pack credits never expire and work across every AI and conversation intelligence feature you switch on.

Use Cases

Never take call notes again

Every call gets a clear AI Note with a summary, chapters and who said what. Your team stays focused on the customer, and anyone can catch up on a conversation in seconds on the web or in the app.

Make sure follow-ups happen

Compliance Notes, and Full Notes on Dubber Notes, pick out the Actions agreed on each call and any Complaints the customer raised. Sales and service teams can act on them straight away.

Stay compliant on every channel

Unified Capture records voice and unified communications to a compliance grade, and Compliance AI adds Notes with Actions and Complaints on top of every recording.

Win more deals and ask your conversations anything

AI Moments such as Sales Close, Sales Process and Customer Satisfaction show how deals and customers are tracking. Insight Agent lets you ask questions in plain language and get insights across your calls.

AI Credit Packs: use cases

Every Dubber Notes and Dubber Notes - Compliance AI user comes with a monthly allowance of AI Credits: 11,100 or 11,700, covering an indicative 7 hours of Notes. An AI Credit Pack gives the account extra AI Credits to draw on once the included allowance for the month is used up. These are the situations where customers buy one.

Teams that spend more time on calls

If the account averages more than 7 hours of Analysed Audio per user each month, a pack covers the difference. On Dubber Notes, recordings beyond 7 hours deduct AI Credits at the recording rate of 1,111 per hour.

Example: 20 users averaging 8 hours each need about 15,560 extra AI Credits a month. One SMB pack covers about 4 months, and one Business pack covers the full year.

Moving busy teams to Full Notes

Full Notes adds Actions and Complaints, which roughly doubles the AI Credits used per hour (about 1,578 compared with 778 for Notes). Sales and service teams that follow up on every call often go past the allowance.

Adding AI Moments and Comprehensive AI

Moments such as Sales Close, Sales Process and Customer Satisfaction use between 27 and 724 AI Credits per hour each. On Notes there's usually room for one or two. For Comprehensive AI, plan on a pack.

Example: 10 users on Full Notes + Comprehensive AI use about 240,520 AI Credits a month, against 111,000 included. Adding a Business pack roughly every 6 weeks keeps them covered.

Insight Agent and custom triggers

These are consumption based, so usage changes from month to month. A pack gives the account a comfortable buffer.

Busy seasons and peaks

Included AI Credits don't carry over from a quiet month to a busy one, but pack credits do, so one pack can see an account through several peaks.

Large compliance teams

Example: 100 users on Dubber Notes - Compliance AI share 1,170,000 AI Credits a month. With Full Notes for everyone, plus Sales Close and Customer Satisfaction for a 30-person contact team, the account runs about 131,370 over. One Enterprise pack covers that for more than 7 months.

How pack credits are used
  • The plan's included allowance is used first. Pack credits are drawn on once it's used up.
  • Pack credits are pooled across every user on the account.
  • They work across all the AI and conversation intelligence features the customer has switched on, including Notes, Actions and Complaints, Moments, Insight Agent and triggers, until the pack is used up.
  • They never expire, and any left over rolls over month after month.
  • Usage shows in the live balance and in the monthly statement, which lists the opening balance, top-ups, usage by feature and closing balance.

6AI Credit Pack top-up notification flow

When an account's AI Credit balance runs low or is used up, the Dubber Notification Centre emails the people the customer has chosen. The email links to the Dubber listing on Verizon Business Marketplace, where they buy an AI Credit Pack. Verizon fulfils the order and Dubber adds the credits to the account's pooled balance.

Step by step

  1. Set up the alert (once). In the Dubber app, an account admin creates an AI Credits email alert, choosing the trigger and the recipients. Alerts can go to more than one person, for example the admin and the billing contact on the Verizon account.
  2. Dubber meters usage. AI Credits are used as Dubber analyses audio, depending on the features switched on. The balance is pooled across the account and is made up of the plan allowance plus any pack credits.
  3. Notification Centre checks the thresholds. An alert fires when a set number of AI Credits has been used, or when the balance falls below the level the customer chose. Default level: [TBD: default low-balance level]. Final "balance used up" alert: [TBD: confirm].
  4. The email goes out. Each recipient gets the low-balance email (mock in section 7). It shows the current balance, recent usage and a suggested pack, with a button to the Dubber listing on Verizon Business Marketplace.
  5. They buy on Verizon Marketplace. The recipient signs in with their Verizon Business account [TBD: permissions needed to buy]. They choose a pack (SMB_A_01, BUS_A_01, BUSP_A_01 or ENT_A_01), add it to the cart and check out. The charge goes on the Verizon bill.
  6. Verizon fulfils the order. Marketplace passes the order to Dubber with the pack part number and the customer's Dubber account reference [TBD: integration method and account matching key].
  7. Dubber adds the credits. The pack's AI Credits go into the pooled balance [TBD: provisioning time]. Pack credits never expire and work across every enabled AI and conversation intelligence feature. The low-balance alert resets.
  8. Confirmation. Verizon sends its standard order confirmation. Dubber sends a "credits added" email to the same recipients with the new balance. The top-up also shows in the live balance and in the next monthly statement, which lists the opening balance, top-ups, usage by feature and closing balance.
