1Introduction

This document sets out Dubber’s proposed gridwall and product page content for the Dubber SKUs on Verizon Business Marketplace. Each tile, section and field follows the structure of existing Marketplace partner listings, so the content can go straight into the Marketplace templates.

SKU part numbers, product names, units, AI Credit inclusions and list prices (US dollars) are taken from the Dubber SKU schedule. Product descriptions are Dubber’s official copy, and usage rates follow the Dubber AI Credits guide. Items we need to confirm with Verizon, such as catalog IDs and commercial terms, are marked [TBD] and listed in section 8.

The document also shows how customers stay topped up without interruption. When an account’s AI Credits run low, the Dubber Notification Centre emails the people the customer has chosen, with a link to the Dubber listing on Verizon Business Marketplace to add an AI Credit Pack. Once the order is fulfilled, the credits are added to the account’s shared balance.

Terminology: “AI Credits” and “tokens” refer to the same unit; customer-facing copy uses AI Credits.

What's covered. The Marketplace SKUs are two base plans (Dubber Notes: Standard and Dubber Unified Capture), one add-on (Dubber Notes: Compliance AI, a bolt-on for Dubber Unified Capture) and four Token Packs (AI Credit Packs). The page follows the Marketplace listing format: gridwall tiles, plan selector, Overview, Benefits, Compare plans, Use Cases and Resources.

2Gridwall tiles

Each gridwall tile shows an optional promo banner, the logo, the product name, a one-line description of about 100 to 120 characters, a “Starting at” price line with “Plus Applicable Taxes and Fees”, and the filter tags (Category and Solution type). The first tile is the recommended Dubber group tile. Below it are the SKU tiles in two groups: the two base plans and the Compliance AI add-on, then the four AI Credit Packs.

Overview

Dubber helps organisations improve sales performance, reduce costs and maintain compliance, with trusted and secure call recording and the AI Notetaker for calls and meetings. Choose a plan below, then add Token Packs when you want more of the insight. Each product description is ready to use as the listing copy for its SKU.

Dubber Notes: Standard (AI Notetaker for calls and meetings) DN_T_01

Not your average AI Notetaker. Trusted and secure call recording combined with Dubber AI’s advanced Notes features, the AI Notetaker for calls and meetings, that allow you to configure call summaries to be sent to people you choose, including chapters, actions and complaints, ensuring they get to the right people so they know what is important on the calls within the business. No bots. Nothing to add to your meetings. There is also the flexibility to upgrade to advanced self-configurable Voice AI features when you need them. Dubber helps organisations improve sales performance, reduce costs and maintain compliance, while providing a secure experience for managing call recordings and advanced Voice AI options. The Dubber AI Portal includes Centralised Storage and tagging capabilities, making recordings easy to organise, search and access securely. Ideal for organisations looking for reliable, secure call recording today, with the flexibility to unlock powerful AI capabilities as their needs evolve.

Configure your AI Notetaker by role and by the outcomes you care about. With easily configurable triggers, notes and alerts can go to a CRO or CEO, a compliance officer, a sales manager or an HR manager when something that matters is said: a sales opportunity, a missed disclosure, an urgent customer request, or inappropriate language, so they get to the right people, the people who can act. Custom use-case configuration comes out of the box, including self-configurable Notes triggers, Sales Playbooks and Service Workflow.

This plan includes 11,100 AI tokens each month, with room to add Sales Playbooks, Service Workflow and other Dubber AI capabilities as you see the value.

Dubber Unified Capture DUR_T_01

Compliance-grade call recording trusted by some of the world’s largest and most security-conscious organisations, with advanced AI capabilities that help you understand what’s being discussed across your business. Combine secure, compliant call recording with configurable AI-powered summaries, actions and insights to help improve sales performance, reduce costs and maintain compliance. The platform provides a broad range of Voice AI capabilities designed to turn conversations into valuable business intelligence, fully configurable by the users. The package includes 7-year retention, team management, SSO, MFA, audit reporting and legal hold, giving organisations the control, security and visibility they need to manage business conversations confidently and at scale.

The Dubber AI Portal includes Centralised Storage and tagging capabilities, making recordings easy to organise, search and access securely.

Dubber is independently certified to ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 22301. Unified Call Recording for Microsoft Teams holds Microsoft 365 Certification. Recordings use AES-256 encryption. Designed to help you meet GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS and MiFID II. See certificates and request documents in the Dubber Trust Centre at https://trust.dubber.net/.

Token Packs (AI Credit Packs): use cases

Every Dubber Notes and Dubber Notes: Compliance AI user comes with a monthly allowance of AI Credits: 11,100 or 11,700, covering an indicative 7 hours of Notes. An AI Credit Pack gives the account extra AI Credits to draw on once the included allowance for the month is used up. These are the situations where customers buy one.

Teams that spend more time on calls

If the account averages more than 7 hours of Analysed Audio per user each month, a pack covers the difference. On Dubber Notes, recordings beyond 7 hours deduct AI Credits at the recording rate of 1,111 per hour.

Example: 20 users averaging 8 hours each need about 15,560 extra AI Credits a month. One SMB pack covers about 4 months, and one Business pack covers the full year.

Moving busy teams to Full Notes

Full Notes adds Actions and Complaints, which roughly doubles the AI Credits used per hour (about 1,578 compared with 778 for Notes). Sales and service teams that follow up on every call often go past the allowance.

Adding AI Moments and Comprehensive AI

Moments such as Sales Close, Sales Process and Customer Satisfaction use between 27 and 724 AI Credits per hour each. On Notes there's usually room for one or two. For Comprehensive AI, plan on a pack.

Example: 10 users on Full Notes + Comprehensive AI use about 240,520 AI Credits a month, against 111,000 included. Adding a Business pack roughly every 6 weeks keeps them covered.

Insight Agent and custom triggers

These are consumption based, so usage changes from month to month. A pack gives the account a comfortable buffer.

Busy seasons and peaks

Included AI Credits don't carry over from a quiet month to a busy one, but pack credits do, so one pack can see an account through several peaks.

Large compliance teams

Example: 100 users on Dubber Notes: Compliance AI share 1,170,000 AI Credits a month. With Full Notes for everyone, plus Sales Close and Customer Satisfaction for a 30-person contact team, the account runs about 131,370 over. One Enterprise pack covers that for more than 7 months.

How pack credits are used
  • The plan's included allowance is used first. Pack credits are drawn on once it's used up.
  • Credits sit in a single shared balance for the whole business, with nothing to allocate user by user.
  • They work across all the AI and conversation intelligence features the customer has switched on, including Notes, Actions and Complaints, Moments, Insight Agent and triggers, until the pack is used up.
  • Unused credits roll over for as long as the agreement runs.
  • Usage shows in the live balance and in the monthly statement, which lists the opening balance, top-ups, usage by feature and closing balance.

6AI Credit Pack top-up notification flow

When an account's AI Credit balance runs low or is used up, the Dubber Notification Centre emails the people the customer has chosen. The email links to the Dubber listing on Verizon Business Marketplace, where they buy an AI Credit Pack. Verizon fulfils the order and Dubber adds the credits to the account's pooled balance.

Step by step

  1. Set up the alert (once). In the Dubber app, an account admin creates an AI Credits email alert, choosing the trigger and the recipients. Alerts can go to more than one person, for example the admin and the billing contact on the Verizon account.
  2. Dubber meters usage. AI Credits are used as Dubber analyses audio, depending on the features switched on. The balance is pooled across the account and is made up of the plan allowance plus any pack credits.
  3. Notification Centre checks the thresholds. An alert fires when a set number of AI Credits has been used, or when the balance falls below the level the customer chose.
  4. The email goes out. Each recipient gets the low-balance email (mock in section 7). It shows the current balance, recent usage and a suggested pack, with a button to the Dubber listing on Verizon Business Marketplace.
  5. They buy on Verizon Marketplace. The recipient signs in with their Verizon Business account [TBD: Verizon account permissions needed to buy]. They choose a pack (SMB_A_01, BUS_A_01, BUSP_A_01 or ENT_A_01), add it to the cart and check out. The charge goes on the Verizon bill.
  6. Verizon fulfils the order. Marketplace passes the order to Dubber with the pack part number and the customer's Dubber account reference [TBD: integration method and account matching key].
  7. Dubber adds the credits. The pack's AI Credits go into the pooled balance. Unused credits roll over for as long as the agreement runs, and they work across every enabled AI and conversation intelligence feature. The low-balance alert resets.
  8. Confirmation. Verizon sends its standard order confirmation. Dubber sends a "credits added" email to the same recipients with the new balance. The top-up also shows in the live balance and in the next monthly statement, which lists the opening balance, top-ups, usage by feature and closing balance.
